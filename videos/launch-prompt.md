# braid launch film

braid runs a team of coding agents on one repo: it plans a goal into a task graph, runs each task in its own git worktree, and merges only what passes your tests.
Plays as a launch video with sound (README, GitHub release, X, Product Hunt). Read `BRAND.md` first.

- 1920x1080, 60 fps, dark. 28 bars at 130 BPM = 51.69 s.
- Music: "Cat Walk", Arulo, Mixkit free license, song from 7.384 s (bass in at film bar 5, full lift at film bar 13).
- Principles taken from the brief (Vidico article): problem first, one core message in the first 10 s ("A multi-agent coding harness for small teams." lands at 6.9 s), the product shown in use, one objection answered (your branches), a clear CTA.
